Gravitational waveswere predicted bygeneral relativitymany years before scientists were able to directly observe them. Significant gravitational waves can only be created by very heavy objects (likeneutron starsorblack holes) moving very quickly. Most such oscillations are binary pulsars whose oscillations createsine waves, which are smooth.

The French mathematicianJoseph Fourierfamouslyprovedthat sine waves of various amplitudes and frequencies can be summed to approximate any periodic function, includingsquare waves. The comic diagram illustrates how a series of binary gravitational-wave oscillators can be stacked to produce a square wave. Square wave gravitational waves would be very unlikely to be found naturally, due to the requirement that multiple independent waves interact in exactly the right way, so observing such waves would surprise scientists.

However, the amount of energy required to manipulate black holes or neutron stars in the way illustrated in the comic is astronomically large, as they usually have masses larger than the Sun. Doing so in a manner to produce a gravitational wave with the level of accuracy to functionally work as a square wave would require energy many tens of orders of magnitude larger than humanity has ever produced.

The title text implies that the waves could be given enough energy to destroy or otherwise significantly affect the Earth.
