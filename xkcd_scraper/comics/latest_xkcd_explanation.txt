ThePerseidsare a meteor shower that appear every August, reaching their highest point on August 12th. This comic was posted a week after then, and hence revolves around missing seeing them.

TheGeminidsare a meteor shower that appears every December, usually peaking around December 14. In North America, since it is relatively cold at night at that time of year, it is not as convenient to view them.

The joke is that the group is using the idea of watching a meteor shower as an excuse for lying on a beach and gazing at the sky, even when it is not actually possible to see them. Even an ordinary sky is full of beauty. Additional humor is added by Megan's comment that the sky has a "normal number" of meteors, which the comic depicts as one. Arguably, any non-zero quantity of meteors is cool. Under good conditions (no light polution), a normal number of meteors in the evening is about 2-4 per hour in March and 4-8 in September. During a meteor shower this can increase to 50-100 at the peak[1].

The title text references the fictional "Daytime Perseids"; Randall mentions not seeing a meteor. This is expected: the light from a burning meteor wouldnormallybe drowned out by the light of the Sun, rendering them unseeable. However, cloudsarevisible during the day, allowing Randall the excuse to watch them instead (and eat snacks).
