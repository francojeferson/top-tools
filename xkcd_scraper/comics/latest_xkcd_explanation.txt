This comic is a play on artistic reconstructions of dinosaurs. Generally, in order to allow viewers to get a concrete idea of the size of the creature, a easily recognizable and sizeable being, like a human or a cat, is placed next to the object, for scale. The scientists studying these pictures, however, seem to have come to the conclusion that the for-scale-creature and the dinosaur were actually in the same environment. This would make the former an easy target for the dinosaur, and hence good prey.

The title text is an extension of the misconception to smaller dinosaurs. Humans have had lots of contact withhousecats,[citation needed]so a cat would serve as a good scale comparison in reconstruction of such a dinosaur.
