This comic lists fifteen films from differentmovie franchises, combined into a single "ultimate movie series". Randall has done so for each entry in the series by comparing the movies that occupy the corresponding slot in their own series and selecting what he considers the best movie. (For example, for the first slot, he would compare movies likeThe Godfather,Star Wars / A New Hope,The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring,Star Trek: The Motion Picture, and so on.) This is absurd due to the movies being unrelated, and especially later on, heavily dependent on previous movies that are not present in the list.

The title text mentionsThe Land Before Timeseries, a franchise consisting of animated adventure films centered around dinosaurs, but states that none of the 14 films would beat out any of the films already listed.  But there is a "devoted fanbase" for the 10th film (The Land Before Time X: The Great Longneck Migration), which thus thinks it should beat out other candidates for the 10th entry.
