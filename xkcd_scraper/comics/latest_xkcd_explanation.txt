In this comic, Cueball has apparently misread the full name of theJupiter Icy Moons Explorer(JUICE) space probe, as he thought the last word was "Exploder". He must have been part of the design team and built something into the probe, and he now needs to update the programming so it doesn't carry out what he thought was the mission to destroy the moons. As of 2026, even the most powerful nuclear devices are not powerful enough to have any meaningful impact on a moon. Why Cueball thought a mission would be funded to explode a moon is unclear, given that it'sunlikely that a probe would be sent out just to blow up moons.[citation needed]

"All systems nominal", standard terminology for spacecraft mission control centers, means that all systems are performing as expected.

In the title text, Cueball is trying to be circumspect about the reason he needs to send a programming update, as he doesn't want to let the rest of Mission Control know about his very odd misunderstanding. It also seems to reveal that the 'exploding' would somehow be accomplished by (mis)using the scientific instruments, perhaps by increasing the energy output of a radar-like instrument to the point that it becomes adirected energy weapon(as seenhere), rather than using the more obvious fuel or power components of the craft.
