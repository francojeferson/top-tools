"Review bombing" is a practice in which a group of people leave negative online reviews to artificially lower the score of a piece of media or property. This practice is most commonly driven by people with an ideological or personal objection to a business. In this case Ponytail explains that her goal is to manipulate state construction projects for her own academic purposes.

By undermining businesses in a specific area, Ponytail hopes to steer business, and therefore traffic, to a different area. This, she assumes, will change the state's road building plans in such a way that they will expose specific rock layers. She specifically refers to"roadcuts"which are sections of hills that are dug or blasted away to make room for a road to go through. Since such cuts make vertical "slices" through the hills, they expose layers of rock, making them useful to geologists who want to examine those rock layers, who would otherwise not have had easy, practical, or legal access to excavate the site themselves. The implication is that Ponytail wishes to study these rock layers but lacks permission and/or funding for a typical excavation projects. Even putting aside the very dodgy ethics of such a plan, it's questionable whether this strategy would work. The comic highlights the somewhat sad truth that basic geologic research is highly dependent on economically driven human activity, especially groundwork. Numeroustype locations(i.e. defining examples for geologic strata) are situated in quarries, digs, etc. This creates multiple problems, because it means that research is often limited to cases where construction work just happens to intersect with significant sites. Even when that does happen, the original purpose of the dig generally takes priority over the research, often leaving geologists and paleontologists to beg for permission to visit sites, work around construction activities, and scurry to secure their finds before the site is destroyed by the same activity that uncovered it.
