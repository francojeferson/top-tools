TheJupiter Icy Moons Explorer(JUICE) is a scientific space exploration spacecraft created by theEuropean Space Agency(ESA).  Its planned voyage began in 2023 and includesflybysofthe Moon(2024),Earth(2024),Venus(2025), Earth (2026), and Earth (2029), before orbitingJupiter(2031) and finally settling into an orbit aroundGanymedein 2034.  The many flybys it will make providegravity assists(25 in total) to help it reach its final destination. Gravity assists work by using the celestial bodies' orbital and gravitational energy (instead of rocket fuel) to accelerate JUICE out of the Sun's gravity well and into Jupiter's gravity well, therefore reducing the amount of rocket fuel needed to reach its mission destination, as well as the mission costs.
